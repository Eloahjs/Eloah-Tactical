Our Story

Built on Purpose.
Rooted in Nebraska.

The name Eloah comes from a Hebrew term meaning strength and singular power — qualities that define how this business operates. Eloah Tactical is a Nebraska-based, owner-operated firearms business founded by John Saulsberry, a certified gunsmith with deep hands-on knowledge across a wide range of firearms platforms.

John's professional background in banking and regulatory compliance shapes how the business is run: with financial discipline, full regulatory adherence, and accountability at every step. We source from the brands you already trust — Federal, Hornady, NORMA, SIG Sauer, Winchester, Traditions, Pedersoli — and deliver them with straightforward pricing and genuine expertise that no online-only dealer can match.

The business is currently relocating within the Lincoln metro corridor, with a property strategy that will support a dedicated gunsmithing workspace and, contingent on zoning, a customer-facing storefront as growth warrants.

Our Story

Built on Purpose.
Rooted in Nebraska.

The name Eloah comes from a Hebrew term meaning strength and singular power — qualities that define how this business operates. Eloah Tactical is a Nebraska-based, owner-operated firearms business founded by John Saulsberry, a certified gunsmith with deep hands-on knowledge across a wide range of firearms platforms.

John's professional background in banking and regulatory compliance shapes how the business is run: with financial discipline, full regulatory adherence, and accountability at every step. We source from the brands you already trust — Federal, Hornady, NORMA, SIG Sauer, Winchester, Traditions, Pedersoli — and deliver them with straightforward pricing and genuine expertise that no online-only dealer can match.

The business is currently relocating within the Lincoln metro corridor, with a property strategy that will support a dedicated gunsmithing workspace and, contingent on zoning, a customer-facing storefront as growth warrants.
