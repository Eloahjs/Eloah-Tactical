Our Story

Built on Purpose.
Rooted in Nebraska.

Eloah Tactical is a Nebraska-based firearms and ammunition supplier built for shooters who take their craft seriously. We source from the brands you already trust — Freedom Munitions, Federal, Hornady, Norma, SIG, Winchester — and make them accessible with straightforward pricing and no-nonsense service.

We're a small operation with a simple mission: get quality ammunition and parts into the hands of responsible gun owners. No fluff. No filler.

Quality
Only trusted, performance-proven brands. No rebranded imports.
Compliance
All sales fully verified and compliant. We do this right.
Value
Competitive pricing on bulk orders, cases, and training ammo.
Service
Real people. Real answers. Nebraska-based and responsive.
