Our Story

Built on Purpose.
Rooted in Nebraska.

The name Eloah comes from a Hebrew term meaning strength and singular power — qualities that define how this business operates. Eloah Tactical is a Nebraska-based, owner-operated firearms business founded by John Saulsberry, with a background in banking and regulatory compliance that shapes how the business is run: with financial discipline, full regulatory adherence, and accountability at every step. We source from the brands you already trust — Federal, Hornady, NORMA, SIG Sauer, Winchester, Traditions, Pedersoli — and deliver them with straightforward pricing and dependable service.

Knowledge
Deep familiarity with the products we carry and the platforms they fit — no guesswork.
Compliance
Full regulatory adherence at the federal, state, and local level — at every stage of growth.
Value
Competitive pricing on bulk orders, cases, and training ammo. No fluff, no filler.
Service
Real people, real answers. Order-on-demand fulfillment with fast turnaround.
