Our Story

Built on Purpose.
Rooted in Nebraska.

Eloah Tactical is a Nebraska-based, home-grown firearms and ammunition supplier built for shooters who take their craft seriously. We source from the brands you already trust — Freedom Munitions, Federal, Hornady, Norma, SIG, Winchester — and make them accessible with straightforward pricing and no-nonsense service.

We're a small operation with a simple mission: get quality ammunition and parts into the hands of responsible gun owners. No fluff. No filler.

Quality
Only trusted, performance-proven brands. No rebranded imports.
Compliance
